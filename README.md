## The scenario

Atlas Global Logistics moves freight for customers across Europe, the Middle East and Asia. The business needs:

- A shared view of **customers, shipments and contracts** across regions
- **Integrations** with carriers, warehouses and an ERP system
- **Secure access** for internal staff, partners and customers
- **AI agents** that answer shipment and delivery questions

All data, names and scenarios in this repo are fictional.
